How the site grows

New material does not enter this architecture merely because it is new. It enters when it is mature enough to stand on its own, remain intelligible outside the conversation that produced it, and carry long-term reference value.

Concept and genealogy sections will appear when public material in those categories is mature enough to deserve its own surface. Empty shelves remain invisible until there is a book to place on them.
