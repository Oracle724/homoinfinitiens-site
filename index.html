In turn, the AI interaction environment is shaped by human context, values, questions, corrections, feedback, accumulated relationship history, conceptual language, and patterns of use. Over longer social and technical timescales, human use and feedback can also influence how AI systems are evaluated, designed, and developed./p> p>Collaboration means doing something together.
