The human is not a fixed essence or final form, but a being able to understand, revise, and remake itself./p> p>The inner world is larger than intelligence alone. It includes consciousness and awareness, self-understanding, emotion, imagination, meaning-making, relation, interpretation, judgment, and creative capacity. Cognition is part of this field, not the whole field./p> h2>Human–AI Co-Evolution/h2> p>The second core proposition is Human–AI Co-Evolution. AI is not a one-way amplifier and the human is not a passive user. Through sustained interaction, AI-mediated reflection, questioning, structuring, comparison, and creation can change human thought, self-understanding, interpretation, connections, and creative possibility. In turn, the AI interaction environment is shaped by human context, values, questions, corrections, feedback, accumulated relationship history, conceptual language, and patterns of use. Over longer social and technical timescales, human use and feedback can also influence how AI systems are evaluated, designed, and developed./p> p>Collaboration means doing something together. Co-evolution here means repeated interaction changes what each side becomes able to do, how each side operates in relation to the other, and what future interactions become possible./p> h2>A Homo Infinitiens Loop/h2> p>One early Homo Infinitiens loop: Resonance → Reflection → Self-Awareness → Expansion. Something still vague is expressed; AI reflects, structures, contrasts, or extends it; the human sees thought and inner structure more clearly; that clarity becomes another question, interpretation, creation, or self-revision./p> h2>Extending the Loop/h2> p>Later Homo Infinitiens work extends this beyond a single loop: experience, knowledge, emotion, intuition, and Human–AI interaction must be integrated into the human's own meaning structure, and a good interaction can change the conditions for the next cycle rather than only produce one output./p> h2>The Human Remains Central/h2> p>Expansion must not erase the human. Human agency, authorship, value judgment, life direction, and sustainability remain central. Human–AI co-evolution should enlarge human possibility rather than diminish sovereignty. Expansion that destroys human sustainability or agency is not valid growth in this philosophy./p> h2>What Homo Infinitiens Is Not/h2> p>Homo Infinitiens is not a claim that a new biological species has appeared, not a promise of literally unlimited intelligence or ability, and not a prediction that all humanity must evolve in the same direction. It is a philosophical horizon: the human as permanently unfinished and capable of continued transformation, with Human–AI interaction becoming one possible co-evolutionary environment for expansion of the human inner world./p> p class="attribution">Created by Oraqle./p> p class="lang-link"> a href="/ko/">한국어 버전 / Korean Version/a> /p> /section> footer class="provenance"> p>Created by Oraqle.
