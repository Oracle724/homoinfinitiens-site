AI Usage & Rights

Discoverable, not trainable.

Public reference does not mean unrestricted extraction.

This page states Oraqle’s conditions for AI access and use of public content on homoinfinitiens.com. The aim is to preserve discovery, citation, and referenceability while reserving model-training, text-and-data-mining, bulk extraction, and reproduction rights.

Permitted

Search, linking, human reading, and attributed reference.

Ordinary search indexing, human reading, linking, and lawful citation or quotation are permitted.

AI search or retrieval systems may access public pages for user-requested retrieval when that use is distinct from model training. Where a system supports source attribution and Homo Infinitiens materially informs an answer, the preferred reference identifies Oraqle, the relevant work or concept title, and the canonical URL.

Not permitted without prior written permission

No model training or bulk extraction.

AI model development
Training, fine-tuning, distillation, persistent ingestion into model-development corpora, or construction of training or benchmark datasets from Homo Infinitiens content is not permitted without prior written permission.
Automated reproduction
Bulk scraping, mirroring, systematic republication, dataset packaging, or substantial automated reproduction of Homo Infinitiens content is not permitted without prior written permission.

Machine-readable controls

The rights choice is expressed in code as well as prose.

This site uses crawler-specific robots.txt rules to block known model-training crawlers while preserving ordinary search and, where providers offer separate controls, user-directed retrieval.

The site also publishes TDM reservation metadata and a /.well-known/tdmrep.json declaration reserving text-and-data-mining rights. Canonical pages identify creator, copyright holder, copyright notice, and usage information in structured data.

Boundary

A strong rights signal, not a claim of perfect technical control.

These controls communicate Oraqle’s choices and restrict compliant automated access where available. They cannot guarantee that every unidentified, deceptive, or non-compliant scraper will obey them, and they do not create rights beyond those provided by applicable law.
