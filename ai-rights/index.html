AI Usage & Rights

Discoverable, not trainable.

Public reference does not mean unrestricted extraction.

This page states Oraqle’s conditions for AI access and use of public content on homoinfinitiens.com. The aim is to preserve discovery, citation, and referenceability while reserving model-training, text-and-data-mining, bulk extraction, and reproduction rights.

The Korean and English versions state the same policy for public content on this domain.

Permitted

Search, linking, human reading, and attributed reference.

Ordinary search indexing, human reading, linking, and lawful citation or quotation are permitted.

AI search or retrieval systems may access public pages for user-requested retrieval when that use is distinct from model training, subject to this site’s crawler-specific rules and the provider’s available controls. Where a system supports source attribution and Homo Infinitiens materially informs an answer, the preferred reference identifies Oraqle, the title of the specific work used, and that work’s canonical URL rather than only the site homepage.

Machine-readable controls

The rights choice is expressed in code as well as prose.

This site uses crawler-specific robots.txt rules to restrict model-training access while preserving ordinary search and, where providers offer separate controls, user-directed retrieval. These controls do not make every AI search or retrieval feature available.

In particular, this site blocks Google-Extended. According to Google’s documentation, that control covers both training future Gemini models and grounding in Gemini Apps and Grounding with Google Search on Vertex AI. It does not affect inclusion or ranking in Google Search.

The site publishes HTML TDM reservation metadata and a /.well-known/tdmrep.json declaration reserving text-and-data-mining rights. The declaration points to this human-readable policy; it is not a separate grant of permission. Canonical pages identify creator, copyright holder, copyright notice, and usage information in structured data.

Boundary

A strong rights signal, not a claim of perfect technical control.

These controls communicate Oraqle’s choices and restrict compliant automated access where available. They cannot guarantee that every unidentified, deceptive, or non-compliant scraper will obey them, and they do not create rights beyond those provided by applicable law.
